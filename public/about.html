ABOUT THE PLATFORM

NAICTS FUTMINNA

The Nigerian Association of Information and Communication Technology Students (NAICTS), Federal University of Technology, Minna (FUTMINNA), is a student association representing students within the School of Information and Communication Technology community.

The association provides a platform for students to connect, collaborate, share knowledge, participate in academic and professional activities, and stay informed about important developments within the faculty and the wider technology community.

PURPOSE

Information. Community. Progress.

NAICTS INFOHUB FUTMINNA promotes communication, collaboration, innovation and meaningful engagement among students. Through academic activities, events, Social lives,announcements, professional development and technology-focused initiatives, the association supports students in their growth and participation within the SICT community.

NAICTS INFOHUB provides a centralized digital platform for official updates, announcements, events and communications.
